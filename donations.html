Charitable Donations

While donations are often optional, giving back helps make a positive impact and can even provide tax benefits. Every little bit counts, whether it’s financial support or your time.

Chosen Organization: Feeding America

About: Feeding America is a nationwide network of food banks that feeds millions of people through food pantries, soup kitchens, shelters, and other community-based agencies. Their mission is to end hunger in the United States.

Why I Chose This: I believe no one should go hungry, especially in a country with so much food waste. By contributing to Feeding America, I’m helping families gain access to nutritious meals, and I feel proud knowing I’m giving back to those who need it most.

Donation Method: I plan to donate $25 per month from my budget to Feeding America. I will also volunteer four hours each month at a local food pantry.

Tax Benefit: Since Feeding America is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, my financial donations are tax-deductible, reducing my taxable income and supporting a good cause at the same time.

Source: https://www.feedingamerica.org
